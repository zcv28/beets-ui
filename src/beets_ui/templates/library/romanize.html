Romanized names

Album, artist and track names become romanized (what Jellyfin shows), and the original names are kept in album_original, albumartist_original, title_original and artist_original. They are written to the files as custom tags, shown under the romanized names here, and searching the library finds either.

  • VocaDB, UtaiteDB, TouhouDB: their Romaji names. Names without a romanization on the site stay as they are.
  • MusicBrainz: the release's Latin-script pseudo-release ("transliterated tracklisting"), when there is one.
  • Albums from other sources, or imported as-is, are not changed.
